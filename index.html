About The Movie

Gotham City

Gotham is consumed by corruption, fear and violence. Batman patrols the streets searching for justice while uncovering secrets hidden deep within the city.

Bruce Wayne

Bruce Wayne is darker, more isolated and obsessed with his mission. After two years as Batman, he still struggles to understand what symbol he must become.

The Riddler

A brilliant and terrifying serial killer who leaves cryptic riddles behind while exposing Gotham's corruption and challenging Batman's detective abilities.

Main Characters

Batman

Gotham's vigilante who uses fear, technology and detective skills to fight crime in the shadows.

Catwoman

Selina Kyle is a mysterious thief searching for revenge while building a complicated connection with Batman.

James Gordon

One of Gotham's few honest police officers and Batman's closest ally during the investigation.

The Penguin

A dangerous crime boss deeply connected to Gotham's criminal underworld and corruption network.
